How I work

  • One accountant. The person you speak with is the person doing the work.
  • Documentary method. Findings come out of the lease and the records. Where the documents do not support a conclusion, the finding says so.
  • Written findings. You receive the analysis in writing, with the reasoning and the supporting schedules, so it can be used in a conversation with your landlord or their accountant.

Where to start

Most engagements begin with a CAM and lease compliance review or with a tax return. Each services page sets out what the work covers and what I need from you.
