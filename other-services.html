General accounting services

Standard accounting work outside real estate accounting and personal tax preparation.

What I do

Standard accounting work, scoped to what the business needs.

  1. Bookkeeping and monthly reconciliations

    Monthly entries, bank and credit card reconciliations, and a month-end close you can report from.

  2. Financial statement preparation

    Profit and loss, balance sheet, and cash flow statements prepared from your records.

  3. Small business tax returns

    Partnerships, S corporations, and LLCs.

  4. Payroll and 1099 coordination

    Payroll runs and year-end 1099s, coordinated with your payroll provider.

  5. Entity selection and setup guidance

    Choosing between an LLC, an S corporation, and a partnership before the formation paperwork is filed.

  6. Budgeting, forecasting, and cash flow analysis

    A forward view of the numbers for a decision you are about to make.

  7. Accounting system setup and cleanup

    Setting up QuickBooks or Xero, or repairing a file that has drifted.

  8. Special projects and one-off financial analysis

    Work scoped to a single question.

Tell me what you need

Describe the work and the deadline, and I will say whether it fits the practice and what it costs.
