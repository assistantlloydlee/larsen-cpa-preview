Privacy

Privacy

This page explains what this website collects and what happens to it.

What the site collects

The site itself sets no cookies and runs no analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts. It loads no third-party fonts or resources, so simply reading these pages does not disclose your visit to anyone other than the host that serves them.

Messages you send

If you use the contact form, the fields you complete (name, email address, phone number if you give one, company if you give one, and your message) are sent to the practice. The form is relayed to the practice mailbox through a third-party form delivery service, which processes the submission in transit. Messages sent by email arrive at that mailbox directly.

The information is used to answer your inquiry and to carry out work you ask for. It is not sold, rented, or used for marketing lists. It is kept for as long as it is needed for those purposes and for the period professional and legal requirements call for, and is then removed.

Confidential documents

Please do not send leases, reconciliations, tax records, or other confidential records through the contact form. Say what you have, and the practice will arrange a secure way to transfer it.

Requests about your information

To ask what information is held about you, to correct it, or to ask for it to be deleted, write to contact@larsen-cpa.com. The practice will respond within a reasonable period and will explain if a legal or professional obligation prevents immediate deletion.
