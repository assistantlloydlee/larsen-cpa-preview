Personal tax

Personal tax preparation for individuals and households

Federal and California income tax returns, prepared carefully and explained in plain language. I work with employees, freelancers, and people with rental or investment income.

Price

Individual returns starting at $200.

The final price depends on the complexity of the return, including additional states, rental properties, or self-employment income.

Who this is for

W-2 employees, people with rental properties, freelancers and self-employed individuals, and people with investment income.

What I prepare

  1. Federal and California individual income tax returns

    Form 1040 and Form 540.

  2. Multi-state returns

    Including New York.

  3. Rental property income

    Schedule E, including depreciation and the records that support it.

  4. Self-employment and freelance income

    Schedule C, with the expense and mileage records behind it.

  5. Investment income

    Stock sales and equity compensation, including RSUs and ESPP.

  6. Estimated tax payment planning

    What to pay during the year, so the following April is not a surprise.

  7. Prior-year and amended returns

    Returns that were not filed, or filed with something wrong in them.

  8. Help responding to IRS and California FTB notices

    Reading the notice and putting the response together.

How it works

  1. 01

    Send your documents

    Last year's return and this year's income documents. The list below covers what to gather.

  2. 02

    Review

    I go through what you sent, ask about anything that is missing, and confirm the scope before starting.

  3. 03

    Draft

    You receive the return as a draft, with the numbers and the changes explained.

  4. 04

    Filing

    Once you approve the draft, I file the return and send you a copy for your records.

What to send

Gather these and send them together. I will tell you if anything else is needed.

  • Last year's filed return.
  • W-2s, 1099s, and K-1s.
  • Mortgage interest and property tax statements, and rental property records if you own property.
  • Records of stock sales and equity compensation, if you have them.
  • Estimated tax payments you made during the year.
